Keep the identity chain

The first-party cookie_id must survive the transition from anonymous prospect to registered lead. Email and postcode are joined to that identifier in the database — never written into the cookie itself.

Keep the cohort

marketing_dimension, strategic_keyword, UTM, POES and acquisition cost travel with the lead so the next experience reflects observed behaviour.

Keep the economics

Once a prospect becomes a lead, the acquisition cost remains attributable. This lets us compare conversion and Customer Equity logic by cohort.

THE CONVERSION DESIGN

Four landing experiences. One product page. One identity chain.

The four pages are not four unrelated campaigns. They are four behavioural doors into the same product decision. Each preserves the original cohort and offers one equal-value benefit under registration.

Price

Free benefit: discount code.

Use value, comparison and economic clarity.

Promotion

Free benefit: special gift / merch.

Use brand value, gifting and exclusivity.

Place

Free benefit: free shipping.

Use convenience and fulfilment reassurance.

Product

Free benefit: experience / route discount.

Use product depth, provenance and discovery.

Recommended classroom architecture: deploy one self-contained index.html with four virtual landing routes (?lp=price, ?lp=promotion, ?lp=place, ?lp=product) plus the final product route (?page=product). Students get five experiences without five Vercel deployments.

METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE JOURNEY

DIIIP explains how behavioural evidence becomes qualified demand

Lead scoring is not the value by itself. Value appears when anonymous data is transformed into permissioned information, intelligence, actionable insight and a personalised next action.

D
Data

Cookie ID, UTM, 4P tags, landing visits, registration, product views, cart, checkout and order events.

I
Information

Lead identity, origin cohort, chosen benefit, consent state, score, recency, cost and current stage.

I
Intelligence

Transparent scoring and milestone rules determine which leads are Cold, Warm / MQL, Hot or Customer.

I
Insights

Which cohorts convert, which incentives work, where leads stall and how acquisition cost behaves by lead stage.

P
Personalization Actions

Cohort-aware nurturing, benefit delivery, product-page configuration and a clear hand-off to conversion or sales.
