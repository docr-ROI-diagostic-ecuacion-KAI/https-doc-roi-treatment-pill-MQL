04 · LEVEL 2 OFFERS · FIRST-PARTY

Decide what happens after each Level 1 click

The first-level banner creates the promise. Here you configure the business action behind it. Product is a contact / advice route; Price changes price or quantity value; Promotion changes the promotional benefit or packaging; Distribution changes delivery or availability. Invalid combinations are blocked.

First-party tracking contract: visitor ID (NEW or SPO), session ID, timestamp, original UTM, Level 1 route, Level 2 activation, Product / Price / Promotion / Distribution frequency, Buy and Request More Information events.
05 · LIVE SIMULATION

Explore Level 1 and Level 2 without losing the customer page

The preview is fully functional in both Mobile and Desktop modes. Every new or returning session starts at the top / head of the customer page. Each Level 1 banner behaves like a curtain: click it and that same page slot becomes Level 2. Each of the four slots is independent, so any combination of Level 1 and Level 2 can remain visible. The Level 2 action can change the Product Sheet dynamically or create the MQL → SQL contact handoff. Use View final page full screen when you want to inspect the customer experience without the laboratory around it.

Simulation mode: legal / cookie acceptance is intentionally hidden inside the preview so it never covers the customer page. A synthetic first-party consent state is applied automatically only for this laboratory simulation; the publishable HTML keeps its real consent logic.
