MQL Campaign & Landing Lab

Plan, execute and inspect Paid and Owned acquisition journeys from campaign configuration to first-party landing behaviour, direct ecommerce purchase or qualified MQL creation — with every qualified lead prepared for SQL review.

01 · DATA FOUNDATION

Load the five evidence files that actually belong to this laboratory

The MQL laboratory receives the Marketing Plan, the Media Content House recipe, Product Experience & Conversion, Buyer Persona · 4P Keywords and the SEM Keyword Plan. No RFM file is requested here. The 40-person Don Espadín classroom cohort is generated internally only when you use Load Example.

Load the five required inputs, or use Load Example from the top of the page.
02 · PAID CAMPAIGN LAB

Configure, buy and execute the Paid campaigns

Each campaign keeps its media spend, CPM, delivered impressions, CTR, UTM, strategic keyword, landing policy and simulated conversion result. Edit the assumptions, then include the Paid stage in the execution queue. The campaigns are not executed until you press Run in Execution & Control.

03 · OWNED / SHARED CONTENT LAB

Prepare the Owned journey and route people through the four marketing-policy landings

Owned and Shared pieces do not add Paid media spend, but they still carry strategic tags, first-party session tracking and a measurable destination. Product is the assisted-sales capture route; Price applies a private-price benefit; Promotion activates gift packaging; Placement activates included delivery.

04 · EXECUTION & CONTROL ROOM

Read the full campaign result before deciding what to keep, scale or stop

The classroom baseline reconciles 40 first-purchase customers from RFM: 15 convert directly in ecommerce and 25 convert after an assisted SQL route. The campaign simulator also generates 10 additional qualified SQL leads that do not purchase, so the commercial path is 35 MQL → 35 SQL → 25 SQL-won purchases. Campaign clicks above these outcomes remain visible as non-converting traffic.

DIIIP · METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE JOURNEY

From campaign data to a qualified commercial action

This laboratory converts campaign and first-party interaction evidence into a traceable MQL decision and a structured SQL handoff.

D
Data

Media spend, CPM, impressions, CTR, clicks, UTMs, first-party identifiers, landing interactions, consent and lead-form responses.

I
Information

The raw evidence becomes a structured journey: source, campaign, content, marketing policy, visitor, session, destination and response.

I
Intelligence

Qualification rules interpret engagement, intent, consent and commercial readiness to distinguish direct ecommerce outcomes from MQL candidates.

I
Insights

The Control Room reveals campaign efficiency, conversion gaps, cost per SQL, ROAS and the Paid activities that should be scaled, continued or paused.

P
Personalization Actions

Qualified leads move to SQL with their acquisition lineage, declared need, preferred contact route and recommended next commercial action.
